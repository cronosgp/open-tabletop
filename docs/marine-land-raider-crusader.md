## Implemented and checked

- The local profile records the one-model composition, 230-point first copy and 250-point second-and-later copies from the source's distinct cost sections. Roster admission and summaries apply the correct tier for each copy; the current local catalog caps this datasheet at three copies.
- The profile retains the source characteristics, keywords, default Hurricane Bolters, Twin Assault Cannon and Armoured Tracks. Optional Hunter-killer Missile, Multi-melta and Storm Bolter choices are reversible during Army Preparation, retain One Shot where sourced, and add no points.
- The shared transport rules allow up to 16 ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY spaces, count GRAVIS and TERMINATOR models twice, and reject JUMP PACK passengers. Damaged 6, Deadly Demise D6, and the sourced transport abilities remain attached to this profile.
- Fury of the Machine Spirit adds Lethal Hits to the Crusader's ranged attacks against a target within 12 inches. The focused attack-plan checks cover the in-range and out-of-range cases.
- Profile, loadout, transport, destruction, admission, saved-roster pricing, Army Builder and Crusader ability regressions passed in the focused offline run recorded in the [machine-readable source and hash audit](marine-land-raider-crusader-audit.json).

Chapter-book content, full army legality, network behavior, complete-match certification and human playtesting remain outside this scope; network validation is deferred to final integration.
