# Current support matrix

Updated 26 October 2026. This matrix describes implemented and admitted scope; it is not a claim that the whole edition or every selectable profile is fully supported. See the [roadmap](roadmap.md) for next work and [verification results](../verification/verification-results.md) for evidence.

**Fist of the God-Emperor (partial):** the 10-point `Oathbound Exemplar` is selectable for current-catalog Adeptus Astartes Infantry identities linked by export row `000011176003`. In a Fist of the God-Emperor match with Black Templars Chapter context, an Advance or Fall Back no longer by itself blocks that unit from starting an otherwise eligible action. OC, Battle-shock, engagement, action timing and other eligibility checks remain in force. The detachment rule, other enhancement and stratagem effects are unavailable. Focused checks, playtesting, network validation and full army legality remain pending.

**Vow-sworn Crusaders (partial):** at the start of battle round 1, a Black Templars player can select one of all three Templar Vows or skip. `Abhor the Witch` grants Precision and Lance to current-reference Adeptus Astartes melee attacks against PSYKER units; `Accept Any Challenge` grants +1 to Wound when target Toughness exceeds attack Strength; `Uphold` secures objectives controlled by current-reference Adeptus Astartes Infantry and lets those units start otherwise eligible actions after Advancing. `Consecrating Aura` (20 points) is selectable for 23 export-linked current-catalog bearer profiles and gives the bearer’s unit a 5+ invulnerable save while preserving better existing saves. `Incendiary Animus` (25 points) is selectable for five export-linked Chaplain or Judiciar profiles and gives the bearer’s unit +1 Strength on melee attacks. `Zealous Vanguard` (25 points) is selectable for 23 export-linked current-catalog bearers and gives the unit Scouts 6 through the shared pre-battle move or Strategic Reserves set-up flow. `Augury Servo-host` (20 points) lets its enhanced unit select one visible enemy within 18 inches during its Shooting phase; the target cannot benefit from cover that phase. `Pious Enmity` (1 CP) is offered when a friendly Chaplain/Judiciar unit is selected to fight in either player’s Fight phase. Choose melee Hit rerolls of 1, or Hit/Wound rerolls of 1 against MONSTER/VEHICLE units. For the Emperor’s Honour! (1 CP) is offered when a friendly Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit is selected to fight in either player’s Fight phase; its melee attacks gain Precision for the phase. Spoor of the Unholy (1 CP) is offered when a friendly Adeptus Astartes unit is selected to attack in its Shooting phase or either player’s Fight phase; the unit ignores BS/WS and Hit modifiers for that phase. Dread Crusaders (1 CP) is offered to the defending Vow-sworn player when an enemy selects a friendly Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit as a charge target; the charging unit makes a Battle-shock roll at -1 through the suspended Charge target-selection flow. Devout Push (1 CP) is offered when a friendly Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit is selected for a Fight-phase pile-in or consolidation; its persisted D3+3 roll drives shared contact placement and remains paid if placement is canceled. Heresy Begets Retribution (1 CP) is implemented as a bounded opposing-Movement reaction: after an enemy move ends within 8 inches, a friendly unengaged Infantry unit that has not moved this phase can roll D6 and make a Surge Move toward a selected closest enemy, with ties offered as choices. The runtime checks move distance, closer/engage requirements, coherency, terrain and bases, and prevents ending engaged with other enemies. Placement currently supports direct paths; it does not solve detours or every coherent alternative around blockers. The 2 DP Force Disposition is unenforced. Current unit abilities and Black Templars Chapter context are required. Focused checks, playtesting, network validation and full army legality remain pending.

**Contact-placement diagnostics:** local Charge, pile-in and consolidation confirmation preserves the first final-validation blocker, anchors feedback to that model, and can list other affected models by roster label and finding in an expandable Command-panel section. The host-to-guest feedback RPC now carries the primary error code, recovery text and additional model findings to the guest. The diagnostics do not alter the result; network round-trip, layout and input remain unverified.

**Multi-storey training terrain:** the local **Multi-storey objective practice** mode selects `terrain-11e-multi-storey-practice-v1`, a project-authored central ruin with separate 3-inch and 6-inch platforms. Movement, contact placement, vertical cost, surface fit, visibility and Plunging Fire use the existing bounded terrain paths. The 6-inch platform is an exercise dimension, not an Event Companion measurement. Focused local checks and human review remain pending; exact imported terrain and arbitrary upper surfaces remain unsupported.

**MONSTER/VEHICLE model passage:** during Normal Moves and Advances, eligible models can pass through friendly and enemy models except other MONSTER/VEHICLE models. The local path check applies this rule per model and move type; endpoint overlap and other move restrictions remain. Charge and Fight movement do not inherit this passage permission. Focused local checks and playtesting remain pending.

**Crushing Impact / Tank Shock:** locally implemented for eligible MONSTER and VEHICLE units after a Charge move. Each selected model's Toughness determines the D6 pool; each 1 damages the charging unit and is allocated by its owner before each 5+ packet (up to six) is allocated by the target's owner. Both use shared mortal-wound prevention and Deadly Demise continuations. This expanded current Wahapedia 11e §15.06 scope has not had focused local checks or human playtesting; network validation and full army legality remain uncertified.

**Shooting at engaged Monsters and Vehicles:** Core Rules §17.03 now limits the engaged-target exception to the controlling player's own Shooting phase, excludes BLAST, applies the -1 Hit modifier with its Close-quarters exception, and keeps an already-engaged shooter restricted to units it is itself engaged with. The path is implemented in shared target selection and attack preparation. Focused local checks and playtesting remain pending; out-of-phase target exceptions, network validation and full army legality are not certified.

**Phobos Shock Force:** `Strike from the Shadows` opens when a hidden friendly PHOBOS unit is selected to attack in Shooting or Fight, spending 1 CP to add +1 Strength and Lethal Hits. `Transhuman Reactions` opens after an enemy move ends within 8 inches of an unengaged PHOBOS unit during the opponent's Movement phase; it spends 1 CP for a D6-inch Normal Move, or a fixed 6 inches with Tactical Doctrine. `Umbral Evasion` opens when an enemy ranged attack targets a PHOBOS unit in the opponent's Shooting phase; it spends 1 CP to give ranged attacks against that unit -1 to Hit for the phase. The 15-point `Seal of Shrouding` is selectable for four export-linked PHOBOS bearers and blocks target selection against that unit during the current Snap Shooting/Fire Overwatch path. The 15-point `Venator Omni-auspex` is selectable for the same four bearers. Against a fully hidden target, its unit re-rolls natural Wound rolls of 1; in Assault Doctrine, it also re-rolls failed natural 2s and offers successful natural 2s as optional free re-rolls. These use persisted reaction choices and shared attack, re-roll or movement paths. Focused local checks and playtesting remain pending; network validation and full army legality are uncertified.

**Phobos Shadow Force:** partial support for all components listed on the current Space Marines 11e page: the `Shadow Masters` detachment rule, `Strike from the Shadows`, `Tactical Withdrawal`, `Mortis Snares`, `Venator Omni-auspex` and `Execute and Redeploy`. After a friendly PHOBOS or SCOUT SQUAD unit shoots in its own Shooting phase, `Shadow Masters` offers two persisted choices: preserve Hidden eligibility for the current turn, or make a D6-inch Normal Move. The shared placement checks per-model distance, terrain, base overlap and coherency; moving blocks Charge until the end of the turn. `Strike from the Shadows` (1 CP) opens when a friendly hidden PHOBOS unit is selected to attack in Shooting or Fight; its attacks gain +1 Strength and Lethal Hits through the shared attack-profile path. `Tactical Withdrawal` (1 CP) at the end of the opponent's Fight phase places one unengaged friendly PHOBOS or SCOUT SQUAD unit with an admitted reserve cost in Strategic Reserves; the existing ingress path handles its later arrival. `Mortis Snares` (1 CP) at Movement-phase end arms every objective currently controlled by one eligible unengaged friendly PHOBOS or SCOUT SQUAD unit. When an enemy ends a move within range of a snared objective, the snare is consumed; its owner rolls D6, and on 2+ a second D6 becomes mortal wounds for the moving unit, allocated with the shared prevention and Deadly Demise paths. Trigger, damage and allocation decisions persist and nest around post-move reactions; reserve setup does not count as a move. Venator is selectable for the same four export-linked PHOBOS bearer identities as the Shock Force version. Against a fully hidden target, the bearer unit re-rolls natural Wound rolls of 1; in Assault Doctrine, rolls of 1 and 2 are eligible, with successful 2s offered as optional free re-rolls. `Execute and Redeploy` is also selectable for those four bearers: after shooting unengaged, it offers a Normal Move up to 6 inches that blocks Charge until turn end. The nested post-shot flow applies Core Rules §09.05.01, so only one Normal Move can resolve in the phase. All listed Shadow Force entries have bounded local paths, but Shadow Masters remains bounded to the owner's Shooting phase. Focused local checks, out-of-phase trigger review, human playtesting, network validation and full army legality remain pending.
