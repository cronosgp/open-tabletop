(1 CP) is offered when a friendly Adeptus Astartes Infantry unit is selected to fight in either player’s Fight phase; its melee attacks gain Precision for that phase. Roll D6, choose the closest Surge target (including ties), and preview a move that preserves coherency, engages the target when the direct path allows, and avoids engagement with other enemies. The local validator supports direct paths and does not solve detours or every coherent alternative around blockers. **Phobos Shadow Force:** partial runtime support covers all components listed on the current Space Marines 11e page: `Shadow Masters`, `Strike from the Shadows`, `Tactical Withdrawal`, `Mortis Snares`, `Venator Omni-auspex` and `Execute and Redeploy`. After an eligible unit shoots in its own Shooting phase, Shadow Masters offers Hidden preservation for the current turn or a D6-inch Normal Move that blocks Charge until turn end. At Movement-phase end, Mortis Snares arms objectives controlled by an eligible unit and creates a persisted trigger when an enemy move ends within range; successful triggers inflict a D6 mortal-wound packet through normal allocation. Venator Omni-auspex is selectable for four export-linked PHOBOS bearers and grants Wound rerolls against hidden targets, including natural 1s and 2s while Assault Doctrine is active; successful 2s are optional free rerolls. Execute and Redeploy is also selectable for those bearers, offering an unengaged 6-inch Normal Move after shooting and blocking Charge until turn end; the post-shot flow enforces the one-Normal-Move-per-phase core limit. All listed current page components have bounded local paths, but Shadow Masters remains limited to the owner's Shooting phase. Focused local checks, out-of-phase trigger review, playtesting, network validation and full army legality remain pending.

**Gauntlet Task Force:** partial `Combined Deployment`, `Linebreaker Onslaught`, `Damocles-class Uplink`, `Storm and Secure`, `Duty is Never Done` and `Aggressive Disembarkation` are selectable. A friendly Astartes Transport can mark one enemy unit it hit after shooting; Astartes units that disembarked this turn gain Sustained Hits 1 against it. Duty is Never Done lets an unengaged Astartes Infantry unit that was eligible to fight this phase embark at Fight-phase end, if every model is within 6 inches of a friendly eligible Transport with sufficient capacity. Other out-of-phase selection contexts remain unimplemented. Focused local checks and human playtesting are pending.

**Chapter Approved mission profile:** each player can select one of the nine implemented 2026-27 primary cards, including Inescapable Dominion, Delaying Action, Immovable Object, Destroyer's Wrath and Outmanoeuvre, and scores that card independently. This uses the Event Companion Mission Generator's direct primary-selection override; the checked Gladius vs Awakened Dynasty profile records each player’s choice between Take and Hold and Priority Assets from its 3 DP detachment; the visible choices are saved, while the primary dropdowns remain direct overrides. Simultaneous hidden reveal, other roster combinations and the complete matchup-derived defaults are not implemented. Both players share one fixed secondary pair: Engage on All Fronts + Assassination (default), Bring It Down, A Grievous Blow, Secure No Man's Land, No Prisoners, Behind Enemy Lines, Centre Ground, Display of Might, Overwhelming Force, Outflank, Forward Position, Defend Stronghold or A Tempting Target. Bring It Down awards 4VP for each opposing W10+ model alive at turn start and destroyed by turn end. A Grievous Blow awards 4VP per opposing unit with battle-start Starting Strength 13+ that was alive at turn start and destroyed by turn end, up to 5VP. Secure No Man's Land awards 5VP at the end of your turn if you control at least two central or expansion objectives. No Prisoners awards 2VP per enemy unit destroyed in a turn, up to 5VP at each turn-end scoring check. Behind Enemy Lines awards 3VP per friendly unit wholly in the opponent's deployment zone, up to 5VP at the end of your turn; AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units are excluded. Centre Ground awards 5VP with an eligible friendly unit within 3 inches of centre and no enemy units within 6 inches, or 3VP if no enemy units are within 3 inches. Its friendly presence excludes AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units. Display of Might awards 2VP at the end of your turn or 5VP at the end of your opponent's turn when you have more eligible units wholly in No Man's Land. Its count excludes AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units. Overwhelming Force awards 3VP per enemy unit destroyed this turn that started the turn within objective range, up to 5VP per scoring check. Outflank awards 3VP for eligible units near an edge outside own territory, or 5VP for units near opposite edges with at least one outside own territory. Forward Position awards 5VP for controlling the opponent's home objective and 5VP for each expansion objective controlled at the end of your turn. Defend Stronghold awards 3VP at the end of your opponent's turn from battle round two if you control your home objective, plus 2VP if no enemy units are within your deployment zone; its first-round replacement draw is unsupported. A Tempting Target awards 5VP at end of turn for controlling the objective selected for you by your opponent after the central-objective configuration roll; the chosen central or expansion objective is shown in mission details. Existing per-turn and per-card caps apply. Both opposing rosters need a qualifying target only for Bring It Down or A Grievous Blow; no-target replacement draws and targets restored or created during a turn remain unsupported. Focused local checks and playtesting are pending.

**Local match continuity:** five save slots are implemented. Slot 1 retains `user://matches/current.save`; Slots 2–5 keep their snapshots and journal branches separately. Before replacing a readable primary snapshot, the writer preserves it as `.bak`; a missing, corrupted or structurally invalid primary can resume from a readable backup, and backup-only slots remain occupied. Newer-version primary saves remain blocked. New local matches select the first empty slot. When all five are occupied, snapshot autosave pauses and the match cannot be resumed until a manual save selects an empty slot or confirms an overwrite; the event journal continues recording history in an isolated directory. Focused save, backup-recovery, resume and branch-isolation checks remain pending because gameplay verification is deferred.

## Product goal

Open Tabletop is an open-source desktop game for two players to play Warhammer 40,000 11th edition matches. The project aims to provide authoritative match state, rules-aware choices and reactions, dice resolution, mission scoring, saved matches, and LAN or direct host connections. Players provide their own miniature visuals. A flexible shared battlefield comes before the final-milestone battlefield editor.

The project has no project-operated server or account service. The local player host is authoritative. Project code and original demo assets are MIT-licensed; third-party rules data and player visuals retain their own source and use terms.

## Decisions that govern the roadmap

- **Rules reference:** Wahapedia's `/wh40k11ed/` rules, faction pages and Data Export are definitive for this project. Preserve the relevant edition, book/chapter, game mode, snapshot and revision. Games Workshop publications may provide context, but are not a prerequisite and do not override this selected reference. See [source assessment](../data/source-assessment.md).
- **Army construction:** expose only profiles in the consolidated local runtime catalog. Do not restore broad API catalog fetching or cached API choices. Pending-validation and historical/unreconciled profiles stay out of new choices; existing affected lists remain labeled legacy drafts. See [construction catalog policy](../construction-catalog-policy.md).
- **Validation order:** continue the Space Marines scope with local rules, combat and admission checks. Defer network validation to final integration; deferred work is never reported as passed.
- **Match flow:** define progression, phase steps, pending decisions, interruption and resumption before broadening timing support. The [phase/action/decision contract](../architecture/phase-action-decision-flow.md) defines this target; it does not claim a generic reaction engine is already implemented.
- **Support claims:** distinguish catalogued, implemented, locally validated, source-checked, network-validated and certified scope. A selectable profile or passing suite alone is not full rules support or army legality.
- **Content distribution:** keep raw Wahapedia exports and derived third-party data local and out of Git/distribution. The clean-checkout demo uses project-owned content and does not silently fetch third-party game data.
