## Represented datasheet

- One Mastodon, M9", T14, Sv2+, W30, Ld6+, OC12; no invulnerable save. It costs 540 points in each of the source's first-, second- and third-copy price tiers. The catalog enforces the standard per-datasheet copy cap: two in Incursion and three in Strike Force. Two copies cost 1,080 points, so they cannot fit in a standard 1,000-point Incursion roster.
- Source keywords: ADEPTUS ASTARTES, TITANIC, SMOKE, IMPERIUM, VEHICLE, FRAME and TRANSPORT.
- Default equipment: one Armoured Tracks, two Heavy Flamers, two Lascannons, one Siege Melta Array and one Skyreaper Battery.
- All seven weapon profiles are represented: Heavy Bolter, Heavy Flamer, Lascannon, Siege Melta Array, Skyreaper Battery, Volkite Culverin and Armoured Tracks, including their source hit skills, range, attacks, Strength, AP, Damage and weapon keywords.
- The two Heavy Flamers may independently be replaced by two Heavy Bolters, two Lascannons or two Volkite Culverins. Separately, the two default Lascannons may be replaced by two Heavy Bolters, two Heavy Flamers or two Volkite Culverins. Both groups may be configured at once and each may be restored to its default.
- Abilities enter the local catalog: Combat Doctrines, Damaged 10, Deadly Demise 2D6 and Inviolable Transport. Focused checks cover the Damaged threshold and the damage-reduction helper; the profile's Deadly Demise value is asserted. Full Deadly Demise timing and all interactions still need integration checks.
- Transport capacity is 45. The source-listed restrictions are represented for ADEPTUS ASTARTES Infantry and up to two Dreadnought models. Jump Pack, Gravis and Terminator models count twice, Centurions count three times, and each Dreadnought model uses its W characteristic in the shared capacity pool. The export's Dreadnought datasheets do not carry a `DREADNOUGHT` keyword, so the current resolver maps the transport term to the eight current catalogued identities listed in the machine-readable audit. This identity mapping remains subject to final live-page/source wording validation.
