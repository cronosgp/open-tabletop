## Player flow and recovery

After Overlord declarations, each player assigns any remaining Leader/Support attachments, chooses a Warlord when Characters are present, optionally declares Terminator reserves, and finishes preparations. Support units require an eligible attachment. Normal deployment follows. Teleport Homers are optional declarations at battle start after deployment, including for Terminators in reserves. Terminator Assault Squad uses the same declaration and ingress flow.

The **Current unit abilities** panel lists legal choices for the current controller. Setup and phase windows must be resolved or declined. X/Z or right-click place Homer/ingress models. Purple arrivals remain previews: every model needs a valid position, bases cannot overlap, the entire unit must be coherent, and every enemy must be more than 8 inches away. Own-Movement arrivals are available from round two. Rapid Ingress is an end-of-opponent-Movement reaction, normally 1 CP or 0 CP using the unit's Homer; the arriving unit must be within 3 inches of that token. Confirmation spends CP/consumes the token once. Cancellation retains reserves and resources. Units that never arrive are destroyed after round three. Reserve allocation uses the configured training-pack budget and its half-limit, not certification of latest official prices.

Units in reserves retain their original wounds/equipment, are hidden, cannot affect movement/visibility/objectives, and cannot attack. An ingress prevents further Movement moves; setup flags reset at the next turn. Homers are visible labels on the board and do not act as model bases.

At phase end, current-profile objective control is resolved before end-phase healing/abilities (core Appendix 14.02.01). At Command start, the active Marine army's doctrine decision precedes opposing optional phase-start abilities. Other optional unit doctrines are selected later during Command. My Will Be Done can be chosen in a stratagem window, including when normal CP are insufficient; all other targeting, Battle-shock and per-phase stratagem restrictions remain.

The 17 October 2026 interaction pass aligned Strategic Acumen and Demi-company Commander with the army-wide doctrine use limit. Gladius Codex Discipline provides the additional use. Guilliman is now selectable at Wahapedia's extracted 415-point cost; his seven abilities and Emperor’s Sword traits are recorded in [the Guilliman implementation note](../space-marines-profile-records.md#marine-guilliman). The current core transcription grants both players 1 Core CP during each Command phase and floors modified stratagem costs at 0 CP. Teleport Homer therefore makes Rapid Ingress cost 0 CP. These interactions remain limited to their implemented scope in the [structured definitions](../space-marines-rule-definitions.json).

All choices are validated against the host's available actions, player and revision before mutation/dice. Setup, Warlords, local/additional doctrines, free reroll checkpoints, limits, tokens and arrival previews are serialized in state. The new `unit_abilities` option and implementation `track-a-current-datasheets-2026-10-v2` enter prepared/saved-list manifests and peer/save checks. Historical saves default to disabled. Clients never supply damage modifiers, profiles or dice results.
