## Implemented scope

Enable **Match setup → Advanced match options → Next prepared match: 11e Combat Doctrines + Space Marine detachment**, then choose the detachment effect. This declares the selected effect for the admitted Marine force; the choice is included in the match identity and both LAN players must agree. Spearpoint also enables current unit abilities because Suboden Khan's end-of-Fight-phase move uses that phase-choice workflow. Current doctrines and the historical Oath option cannot be enabled together. The checked saved-roster snapshot and selected CA mission remain Gladius-only. The older Oath profile remains historical training code and is not current-rule certification.

With current unit abilities enabled, Advanced match options also offers a per-player **Chapter context**. Leave it on Infer to use the current roster's named Chapter, then the selected Chapter restriction for Wrath of the Rock or Spearpoint; choose a Chapter explicitly to apply its context to generic current-reference Adeptus Astartes profiles. A named roster identity or detachment conflict is rejected during preparation. The source-linked Dark Angels conditions grant DEATHWING to Terminator/Dreadnought and listed Veteran/vehicle identities, and RAVENWING to Mounted and Vehicle+Fly identities. These runtime keywords do not define the army's Chapter. Context and the selected declarations are kept in the match manifest and saved army setup. Focused local checks remain pending, and this feature does not certify full Chapter or army legality.

At own Command start, choose a doctrine or explicitly choose none. The choice precedes core CP gain and Battle-shock. Choosing or declining costs no CP or dice; the subsequent normal core CP is gained once. Declining preserves uses. A doctrine lasts until the start of the next own Command and remains active during the opponent's turn. Expiry happens before choosing again.

- **Devastator:** ranged attack profiles receive Assault. The effective profile is a copy; original weapons are unchanged. It enables the existing e11 assault-shooting path, retaining its engagement, range, visibility and weapon-selection restrictions.
- **Tactical:** Fall Back does not block shooting or charge declaration. Actual Fall Back movement, leaving engagement, hazards where applicable and other eligibility still resolve normally.
- **Assault:** Advance does not block charge declaration. It does not grant the Assault weapon keyword or permit unrelated shooting.

Each doctrine has one normal use. Codex Discipline supplies **one shared additional use**, spent when a previously selected doctrine is selected a second time. It does not grant one extra use of every doctrine or a third use of one doctrine. The selector marks choices that spend this extra use and shows whether it is spent. After all uses are exhausted, Command proceeds without an empty mandatory choice. The engine also tests the army rule without the Gladius effect; the current prepared-match option explicitly selects Gladius.

These doctrine effects come from the pinned local Wahapedia e11 extraction. They apply to the admitted Marine force, not the opponent or a foreign/mixed faction. They change shooting/charge eligibility only; actions, existing action commitments, engagement, charge range, once-per-phase use and placement requirements still apply. The shared query supports simultaneous doctrine effects: `codex-adept` keeps all three doctrines active for Guilliman; `author-of-the-codex` grants one doctrine to a visible friendly Astartes unit within 12 inches during Movement until the next own Command; and Master of the Machine War adds a selected Devastator or Tactical Doctrine to a visible friendly Astartes Vehicle within 6 inches of its bearer until the next own Command. Its selection uses the shared once-per-battle doctrine limit. `primarch-of-the-xiii` grants nearby friendly Astartes hit/wound re-rolls of 1 within 6 inches. Guilliman's other implemented effects and selectable profile are recorded in [his implementation note](../space-marines-profile-records.md#marine-guilliman).

## Blade of Ultramar

The Wahapedia Space Marines 11e App 2.8.1 page, observed 26 October 2026, defines **Mastered Doctrines**: with a MARNEUS CALGAR unit in the army, each of the three Combat Doctrines can be selected one additional time per battle. The current runtime keeps the extra use separate for each doctrine and requires Blade of Ultramar with Ultramarines Chapter context.

**Student of the Codex** (20 points), **Armour of Antoninus** (15 points), **Oath of Macragge** (20 points) and **Veteran of Behemoth** (20 points) are selectable in the partial detachment. Student of the Codex is limited to six current-catalog Captain profiles and makes Tactical Doctrine active for the bearer's unit alongside its other doctrine. Armour of Antoninus is limited to 23 source-linked current-catalog Character profiles; its bearer has a 2+ Save and Feel No Pain 5+ against any wound. The enhancement uses the existing per-model Save and wound-prevention paths, so its defensive effects do not spread to other models in an attached unit. Oath of Macragge is selectable for 23 source-linked Character profiles; its bearer's melee attacks gain +1 Strength and AP, or +2 Strength and AP while Assault Doctrine is active. Veteran of Behemoth is selectable for the same 23 Character profiles and grants Sustained Hits 1 to the bearer unit's ranged attacks. The local export links rows `000010633003`, `000010633004` and `000010633005` to the same 37 datasheets, with 23 resolving to current catalog Character profiles.

All four enhancements and all six stratagems now have bounded local runtime paths. Armour of Contempt binds -1 AP to the triggering enemy’s attacks. Courage grants Lance to the selected unit’s melee attacks in the Fight phase. Exemplary Vigilance grants ranged attacks Ignores Cover. Ultramarian Adaptivity assigns one Combat Doctrine to a friendly unit until its owner’s next Command phase. Practical Tactics offers an unengaged eligible unit a D3+3-inch Normal Move after an enemy move ends within 8 inches. Tactical Foresight offers a defender-owned choice at attack declaration and applies -1 to Wound only when the triggering enemy’s Strength exceeds the selected unit’s Toughness. Focused local checks, current official rule locators, network validation and full Ultramarines army legality remain pending. ## Authority and recovery

Implementation ID: `combat-doctrines-11e-gladius-v1`. Owners, declared detachment, active doctrine, selection turn, spent selections, shared extra-use flag and deferred core-CP step are public serializable state. Gladius retains the prior match identity and default for older saves; Stormlance adds its detachment ID to the match manifest. Setup/saved-list options freeze the selection. Disabled matches retain their previous pack identity, and inconsistent save/peer flags are rejected.

The host validates phase, active player, revision, doctrine and remaining uses. Clients send doctrine IDs rather than effects or dice. While a choice is pending, gameplay commands and tactical destinations are blocked. Save/load and authenticated rejoin after restarting the host preserve the pending choice and CP step. Selecting after restoration consumes no dice or duplicate CP. Effective ranged weapons and frozen attack plans continue through the existing fast and staged attack paths.
