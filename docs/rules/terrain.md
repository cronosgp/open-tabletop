# Ground terrain in the 11e exercise

## Event Companion terrain presets

The selected **Battlefield Dominance / Take and Hold vs Take and Hold** match offers the three matching 11th-edition Event Companion layouts: **A, B and C**. These are terrain-only presets. The selected mission, its objective locations, Hammer and Anvil deployment, and scoring stay unchanged. The prepared match also offers Meatgrinder, Reconnaissance Sweep and Secure Asset, but currently reuses these Take and Hold terrain proxies for those primaries; their own recommended Event Companion layout pairs are not implemented. This is not the older A/B/I/J mission pool.

The preview images are the 11e Wahapedia Event Companion diagrams: [Layout A](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/img/maps/layouts/43_Take_and_Hold_vs_Take_and_Hold_Layout_A.png), [Layout B](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/img/maps/layouts/44_Take_and_Hold_vs_Take_and_Hold_Layout_B.png), and [Layout C](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/img/maps/layouts/45_Take_and_Hold_vs_Take_and_Hold_Layout_C.png), listed in the [11e Warhammer Event Companion](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/warhammer-event-companion/). They are written to `.tools/wahapedia/11e/event-layouts/take-and-hold-mirror/`, which is ignored by Git and excluded from distribution. The match never downloads them automatically, and peers do not receive them. ## First-floor and multi-storey movement and visibility training

The default `terrain-11e-v1` layout has a project-authored 3-inch first floor in its central ruin. Event Companion layouts A, B and C also give each Dense ruin a bounded first-floor surface at 3 inches. The Event Companion diagrams show ground layouts but do not specify ruin footprints or floor measurements; these added floors are project-authored training proxies using the Core Rules' general 3-inch first-floor convention. They do not certify the exact construction or measurements of a physical terrain set.

The optional local **Multi-storey objective practice** mode selects `terrain-11e-multi-storey-practice-v1`. It keeps the central ruin's 3-inch floor and adds a smaller 6-inch second-floor platform, with the ruin walls extended to that height. The two platform dimensions are project-authored exercise geometry; the 6-inch floor is not claimed as a standard or Event Companion measurement. The ordinary default and Event Companion layouts remain unchanged.

Movement and contact-placement flows allow eligible Infantry, Beasts, Swarm and FLY models to finish on a supported surface when their whole base fits. The selector offers destination heights; the chosen map position resolves which floor at that height the model occupies. Ordinary vertical moves stay within 1/2 inch horizontally of a supported ruin wall and count vertical distance. Infantry, Beasts and Swarm can move vertically through Dense terrain; FLY models use ordinary terrain access and pay vertical distance unless they declare Take to the Skies. Equipped Reiver units with Grapnel Launchers retain wall access and ignore vertical distance. When a FLYING unit declares Take to the Skies for a Normal Move, Advance, Fall Back or Charge, its FLY models can pass through all models and terrain while ignoring vertical distance; subtract 2 inches from the unit's maximum distance unless it has HOVER. During Charge, Pile-in and Consolidation placement, choose the destination height per model; each staged segment checks wall access, remaining distance, base fit, coherency and contact requirements.

Bounded 3D cylindrical-proxy visibility supports ground-level and elevated models on these surfaces. Visible ranged attacks from a model at least 3 inches above ground can use Plunging Fire against a target unit containing a ground-level model. TOWERING models can use the rule against visible targets within 12 inches. AIRCRAFT are excluded. Core Snap Shooting requires an unmodified 6 regardless of BS, except where another rule sets a different threshold. The rule boundary follows the current [11th-edition Core Rules](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/core-rules/): Wahapedia identifies the rulebook date as June 2026, and the page was observed on 26 October 2026. Relevant rules cover visibility (06.01), terrain visibility (13.07, 13.10–13.11), vertical terrain movement (13.06), Snap Shooting (15.09), Plunging Fire (22.05), AIRCRAFT (23.03), Charge (11.04), coherency (03.03), Engagement Range (03.04), FLY (21.03), and HOVER (24.17).

The local movement, contact, coherency, engagement and objective checks account for height: coherency uses the 5-inch vertical limit alongside its 2-inch and 9-inch horizontal limits; Engagement Range uses 5 inches vertically and 2 inches horizontally; objective markers use 5 inches vertically and 3 inches horizontally. Terrain objectives continue to use their full terrain area, including supported upper surfaces. Bounded 3D cylindrical visibility rays between supported surfaces apply area obstruction, physical feature heights and the 1 mm sightline width. Unsupported surfaces still return an explicit geometry blocker. Plunging Fire improves BS by 1 when an eligible attacker at least 3 inches above ground shoots a visible unit that contains a ground-level model; TOWERING can qualify within 12 inches, and AIRCRAFT are excluded. During Core Snap Shooting, hits still require an unmodified 6 regardless of BS, except where another rule sets a different threshold. MONSTER terrain paths, upper surfaces beyond the fixed practice heights, arbitrary vertical paths, exact imported-mesh visibility and the battlefield editor remain unsupported. Normal Move and Advance model-passage rules for MONSTER/VEHICLE are documented separately in [movement choices](movement-types.md). Focused local checks and playtesting remain pending; this increment has not been verified in Godot.

The combat exercise defaults to `terrain-11e-v1`: a dense central ruin, exposed rubble and a light barricade. The original prototype keeps its demonstration terrain. Map geometry is part of the combat pack identity; peers with different layouts cannot share a match.

Infantry, Beasts, Swarm and Mobile models can cross dense walls horizontally. Infantry, Beasts and Swarm models can move vertically through dense terrain. FLY models only gain unrestricted terrain and model traversal when they declare Take to the Skies for an eligible move; otherwise they follow their ordinary terrain permissions. Equipped Reiver units with Grapnel Launchers can pass through models and ignore vertical distance, but still use the normal terrain access path. Other models cannot cross dense sections taller than 2 inches at ground level in this bounded proxy. Light and exposed features permit traversal. Models cannot finish embedded in a wall or barricade. The rendered cylinders are the gameplay visibility volumes.

Cover is evaluated separately for each attacking model against every surviving model in the defending squad, before attacks resolve. A defender qualifies by being Infantry/Beasts/Swarm within a terrain area, or by being partly hidden by terrain. Cover worsens BS by 1, capped at 6+, rather than improving armour saves. Heavy remains a separate hit modifier. Identical attacks split into pools when cover or Rapid Fire eligibility differs. Ignores Cover suppresses the BS penalty without bypassing visibility or detection.

Infantry, Beasts and Swarm models within a terrain area containing a Light or Dense feature can be Hidden if they have not fired this turn or the previous turn. A unit counts as Hidden when at least one of its models is Hidden. Detection uses base-edge distance, normally 15 inches. Hidden models partly concealed by dense physical features benefit from Gone to Ground, reducing detection to 12 inches. Shooting records the firing turn even when every attack misses. Light and dense areas can block sight across the area unless either model is within that area; physical walls can still block sight within an occupied area.

This follows the current [Core Rules, 13.09 Hidden](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/core-rules/) and the [Space Marines page, Phobos Shock Force](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/). The geometry implementation remains a local proxy.

## Geometry limits

Visibility uses cylindrical proxies. Ground-level pairs search nine witness rays; pairs involving a supported elevated surface sample 36 combinations of horizontal offsets and model base/top heights, enforcing the 1 mm line width and feature heights. One clear sampled ray authorizes visibility. Full visibility still uses a projected hull approximation, and these proxies do not reproduce every facing part of an imported miniature. Supported floors are limited to the default 3-inch central-ruin floor, the optional practice layout's 3-inch and 6-inch floors, and the Event Companion A/B/C Dense ruin proxies. Arbitrary imported terrain and a player battlefield editor remain unimplemented. The bounded contact solver also retains its documented unresolved approach exceptions.

## Verification

`tests/test_terrain.gd` checks traversal, illegal endpoints, cover pools and BS limits, Hidden timing, Light/Dense Hidden eligibility, detection boundaries, Obscuring, Gone to Ground and contact certificates using traversable ruins. `tests/test_vanguard_ambushers.gd` checks the blocked capability gate, the current-turn Shooting/Fight predicate, expiry, detachment/phase boundaries and Wound rerolls of 1; it does not validate unit-level Hidden eligibility, the Movement-end trigger or complete attack integration. `tests/test_terrain_network.gd` checks guest movement, shooting, replicated cover/history, guides and stale requests on the new map. Older geometry fixtures explicitly select `legacy-solid-v1` to retain their original obstacle assertions.

Prior Windows validation passed for eleven local rules/UI suites and eight two-process host/client suites. Those reports predate vertical contact placement, the revised FLY traversal gate, elevated visibility and Plunging Fire; focused checks remain pending. Loopback does not verify separate-machine LAN or Linux.
