# Fist of the God-Emperor

Fist of the God-Emperor is a **partial local implementation** of the 11th-edition Black Templars detachment. It requires current unit abilities and Black Templars Chapter context. The checked Chapter Approved roster profile records its 1 DP cost and permits **Take and Hold only**. This does not claim complete army legality or complete detachment support.

## Crusader Squad and Purge and Sanctify

The current-reference Crusader Squad uses the local export datasheet `000002799`, with supported 10- and 20-model compositions. The export-defined Sword Brother, Initiate and Neophyte loadouts are built per model; Righteous Zeal is admitted as the squad's unit ability.

After an enemy unit shoots in the opponent's Shooting phase and destroys a model in an eligible Crusader Squad, the squad can make a Surge move up to D6+1 inches. The local flow offers use/pass, rolls D6+1, then asks the controlling player to select a closest enemy target, including a tie choice. Placement enforces the shared terrain and base checks, rolled movement limit, coherency, closer/Engagement Range destination rules, and the rule against ending engaged with a different enemy. A bounded waypoint search supports detours around terrain and blocking bases, with every segment checked by shared movement geometry; finite route sampling does not guarantee that every legal route or coherent placement is found.

`Purge and Sanctify` (detachment ability row `000011175`) lets a Crusader Squad select a closest objective instead of a Surge target. The placement flow checks the objective-range and as-close-as-possible outcomes through the bounded waypoint search. Enemy Engagement Range destinations are now excluded using exact intersections of the final route segment with the engagement circles, including narrow legal gaps between multiple enemies. Route vertices and joint coherent alternatives remain bounded and are not exhaustive. For current-reference Marine profiles, prepared-match admission applies the single-Chapter gate: shared Codex profiles can use the selected Black Templars context, while a roster containing a profile from another Chapter is rejected. This gate does not certify legacy or external roster sources, or complete army legality.

## Enhancements and stratagems

`Righteous Fervour` (15 points, enhancement row `000011176002`) is selectable for the Crusader Squad. It provides a free Advance re-roll through the existing post-roll decision. `Oathbound Exemplar` (10 points, row `000011176003`) remains selectable for its export-linked Adeptus Astartes Infantry profiles; Advancing or Falling Back does not by itself prevent the unit from starting an otherwise eligible action.

- `Doctrinal Flexibility` (1 CP) is available in the owner's Command phase for a friendly Crusader Squad. It selects an unused Combat Doctrine for that unit until the start of the owner's next Command phase and records the army-wide doctrine use.
- `Angels Defiant` (1 CP) is available when an enemy targets a friendly Crusader Squad in the opponent's Shooting phase or either player's Fight phase. Attacks with Strength greater than the unit's Toughness worsen their Wound roll by 1 for that attack sequence.
- `Avowed Destruction` (1 CP) is available when a Crusader Squad is selected to fight. Its melee attacks gain either Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1 for the Fight phase.

The prepared-match selector requires Black Templars context. The current-reference Chapter gate is covered by a focused admission check. Checked Gladius / Fist versus Awakened Dynasty setup carries the selected detachment into roster admission; Fist stores its 1 DP cost, and its only available Force Disposition is enforced in direct setup and sealed local selection. Focused profile and selector checks pass in `.tools/verification/20261028T153543222779Z/results.json`; they exercise the rule helpers, not a full saved-roster launch or visual selection. The route regressions passed in `.tools/verification/20261029T075602211222Z/results.json` (6/6 processes, `network: false`), including the narrow safe endpoint interval between enemy engagement circles. Finite route vertices, coherent-placement search, network validation, human playtesting and complete army legality remain open.
