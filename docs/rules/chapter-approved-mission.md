# Selected Chapter Approved 2026-27 mission (A2)

Enable **Advanced match options -> Next prepared match: Chapter Approved 2026-27 mission**, choose one supported primary for each player, then prepare a match or select both saved lists. Both dropdowns start on the same card. This enables the current unit-ability/faction/core-stratagem options. For saved lists, this profile admits Space Marines as P1 and Necrons as P2 with an Incursion category and a 1,000-point limit. Their pinned costs and full army construction remain unverified (A3).

## Selected scenario

The setup supports eight current primary cards, selected independently for each player: **Battlefield Dominance** and **Immovable Object** (Take and Hold), **Meatgrinder** and **Destroyer's Wrath** (Purge the Foe), **Outmanoeuvre** and **Delaying Action** (Disruption), **Reconnaissance Sweep** (Reconnaissance), and **Secure Asset** (Priority Assets). Selecting the same card keeps the former mirrored practice. Each player scores their own card; Secure Asset is offered only to the player who selected it. Deployment remains **Hammer and Anvil**. Both players use one shared fixed pair: **Engage on All Fronts** + **Assassination** (default), **Bring It Down**, **A Grievous Blow**, **Secure No Man's Land**, **No Prisoners**, **Behind Enemy Lines**, **Centre Ground**, **Display of Might**, **Overwhelming Force**, **Outflank**, **Forward Position**, **Defend Stronghold**, or **A Tempting Target**. No Twist or Tactical secondary deck is selected. This remains a bounded mission profile, not a random mission/deck generator. Setup offers the three 11th-edition Event Companion Take and Hold mirror terrain presets A, B and C. The same terrain proxies can be selected with the other primaries, but they are not represented as their recommended layouts; see [ground terrain](terrain.md).

The fixed **Bring It Down** card awards 4VP for each enemy model with 10 or more Wounds destroyed during the turn, up to 20VP on the card and subject to the mission's secondary caps. Both players check their card at the end of each turn. The local tracker snapshots living models at turn start and checks which are destroyed at turn end. To keep the fixed pair available to both players, each opposing roster must contain at least one model with 10 or more Wounds. If a roster has no such model, setup returns an error instead of silently replacing the card. The source's no-target discard-and-draw instruction, models restored during the turn, and newly created W10+ models are outside this implementation. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **A Grievous Blow** card awards 4VP for each enemy unit with a Starting Strength of 13 or more destroyed during the turn, up to 5VP on the card and subject to the mission's secondary caps. Both players check their card at the end of each turn. The implementation records Starting Strength after deployment and pre-battle setup, then checks units alive at turn start that are destroyed by turn end. Each opposing roster must contain at least one 13+ model unit or setup returns an error. The source's no-target discard-and-draw instruction and targets created or restored during the turn are outside this implementation. The exact scoring conditions are in the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Secure No Man's Land** card awards 5VP at the end of your turn if you control at least two objectives in No Man's Land, subject to the mission's secondary caps. In this Hammer and Anvil profile, the central and expansion objectives are in No Man's Land; the attacker and defender home objectives are excluded. Only the active player's card is checked at the end of that player's turn. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **No Prisoners** card awards 2VP for each enemy unit destroyed during the turn, up to 5VP at that turn's scoring check and subject to the mission's secondary caps. Both players check their card after each turn. The local tracker uses the mission's turn-start and turn-end unit-loss snapshot; restored units are outside this bounded implementation. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Behind Enemy Lines** card awards 3VP for each friendly unit wholly within the opponent's deployment zone at the end of your turn, up to 5VP for that scoring check. AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units are excluded. The local check requires every living model's base to fit inside the recorded opponent deployment zone. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Centre Ground** card awards 5VP if one or more eligible friendly units are within 3 inches of the battlefield centre and no enemy units are within 6 inches. Otherwise, it awards 3VP if eligible friendly presence is within 3 inches and no enemy units are within 3 inches. AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units cannot establish friendly presence. Enemy units, including AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units, still deny the space when within the stated distance. The local distance check measures from the closest base edge and includes model elevation above the battlefield. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Display of Might** card awards 2VP at the end of your turn or 5VP at the end of your opponent's turn if you have more eligible units wholly within No Man's Land than your opponent. AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units are excluded. For this Hammer and Anvil profile, No Man's Land is the central strip between the two deployment zones. The local check counts a unit only when every living model is on the battlefield and its base fits wholly inside that strip.

The fixed **Overwhelming Force** card awards 3VP for each enemy unit destroyed during the turn that started the turn within range of one or more objectives, up to 5VP for that scoring check. The local tracker snapshots enemy units within the current objective-range rule at turn start and checks whether each such unit is destroyed at turn end; units restored during the turn are outside this bounded implementation. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Outflank** card awards 3VP if one or more eligible friendly units are within 6 inches of a battlefield edge and outside your territory. It awards 5VP instead if two or more eligible units are within 6 inches of opposite edges and at least one of them is outside your territory. AIRCRAFT and Battle-shocked units are excluded. The local check measures edge distance from the nearest base edge and treats a unit as within its territory if any living model's base overlaps that player's half of the battlefield; the second scoring condition can include one unit inside that territory when another qualifying unit is outside it. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Forward Position** card awards 5VP for controlling the opponent's home objective and 5VP for each expansion objective you control at the end of your turn. If you control both the opponent's home objective and both expansion objectives, the card can request 15VP before the existing per-turn and per-card caps. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **Defend Stronghold** card scores from battle round two at the end of your opponent's turn: 3VP if you control your home objective, plus 2VP if no enemy units are within your deployment zone. The local check treats an enemy unit as within the zone if any living model base overlaps it. Its first-round draw replacement is not automated in this fixed-pair profile, so it does not score in round one. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

The fixed **A Tempting Target** card has each opponent choose one central or expansion objective for the other player after the mission rolls the central-objective configuration. It awards 5VP at the end of your turn if you control the chosen objective. The target remains listed in the mission summary and objective result details. See the [Wahapedia 11e Chapter Approved mission deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/).

**Destroyer's Wrath** awards 3VP at the end of your turn if one or more enemy units were destroyed. From battle round two, its checkpoint awards 4VP if you control one or more non-home objectives and 6VP if you control more objectives than your opponent. At the end of your turn from battle round two, it also awards 4VP if more enemy units were destroyed this turn than friendly units were destroyed in your previous turn. Existing primary scoring caps apply. See the [Wahapedia 11e Event Companion, version 1.3](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/warhammer-event-companion/).

**Outmanoeuvre** awards 10VP at end of turn for controlling the opponent's home objective. It also awards 4VP per non-home objective at the end of battle round one, 5VP per non-home objective at the end of the Command phase in rounds two and three, and 6VP per non-home objective at end of turn from round four onwards. Existing primary scoring caps apply. Its scoring uses the current objective controllers and the player's attacker/defender home role.

**Immovable Object** awards 3VP at the end of any turn when you control one or more central objectives. At the end of the Command phase in battle rounds two to four, and at the end of your turn in battle round five, it awards 5VP per objective you control excluding your home objective. Existing primary scoring caps apply.

**Delaying Action** awards 2VP for each enemy unit destroyed at the end of any turn. From battle round two, it also awards 4VP at the scoring checkpoint if you control at least one objective other than either home objective, and 3VP at the end of your turn if you control both a central and an expansion objective. Existing primary scoring caps apply.

The board is **60 x 44 inches**, with the long dimension along engine X. Attacker deploys wholly in X -30 to -12; Defender in X 12 to 30. The five objective points, expressed as engine (X,Z), are Attacker home (-16,-12), Defender home (16,12), expansions (-6,14) and (6,-14), and central (0,0). A D6 of six replaces the central point with (0,-6) and (0,6). The custom agreed terrain uses a 6 x 4-inch light terrain area covering each point with a low barricade proxy. These **terrain areas** are the objectives, using existing e11 terrain-objective control; they are not old-edition circular objective markers. The selected Event Companion layout adds ground-level proxy terrain that avoids these objective areas; proxy geometry and visibility have the limitations documented in [ground terrain](terrain.md).

The preset map and custom terrain are the agreed scenario configuration. With the checked Gladius vs Awakened Dynasty roster profile, setup offers each player the two Force Dispositions available from that roster's single 3DP detachment: **Take and Hold** or **Priority Assets**. Both selections are saved in the match manifest and shown in the mission overview. Their availability is drawn from [Wahapedia’s Space Marines](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/) and [Necrons](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/necrons/) pages; the direct override is defined by the [2026-27 Mission Deck](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/). The primary dropdowns continue to use the Event Companion Mission Generator's direct-selection override, so their chosen cards determine scoring even when they differ from the Force Disposition matchup. The choices are visible during local setup; simultaneous hidden selection and reveal, and the complete set of 25 matchup-derived defaults, are not implemented. Other roster/detachment combinations do not expose Force Disposition choices. Tactical secondary decks, twists, and all other primary cards are outside this profile. The seven additional primary cards use their listed scoring conditions with the selected fixed pair and map/deployment profile. This is not a claim that every possible Chapter Approved configuration is implemented.

Reconnaissance Sweep's end-battle 10VP check uses the Event Companion definition of territory: the half of the battlefield containing that player's deployment zone. Missing deployment-zone geometry fails closed and does not award that bonus.

## Preparation and recovery

Resolve central-objective configuration, then a role roll-off. Each roll-off rolls once for each player; ties require another explicit roll. The winner chooses Attacker or Defender. Declare each army's Battle Ready status; no painting VP is granted yet. Resolve the current Leader/Support/Warlord preparations and reserve declarations, then alternate deployment starting with the Defender. Both players explicitly accept or place coherent previews and confirm each unit. Previews account for different base sizes and attached components, avoiding terrain walls and earlier suggested units.

Reserve declarations are concealed in the guest's state, model flags, deployment omissions/order and event log until both sides commit formations. Remote history is withheld during that interval to prevent a history-based leak. The authoritative host retains the full state for saving. This assumes the existing trusted host model and does not protect against a modified host inspecting its local save.

After deployment, roll off for first turn; the winner goes first. Existing pre-battle Homer declarations follow in first-player order. A fresh host can resume any pending mission choice; both primary assignments and the implementation ID enter the public setup/manifest, save/config checks and peer compatibility. The ID is `ca-2026-27-dominance-hammer-fixed-v1`. Both LAN peers must agree on both primary assignments. Historical saves default to disabled and keep their training identities; old saves of this mission restore the former shared card for both players.

## Scoring

Battlefield Dominance grants the current card's objective-control and home-objective cumulative bonuses. Rounds 1-2 also check the end-turn control advantage. Rounds 2-4 score control at own end of Command; **both** players defer that control scoring to own end of turn in round 5. Meatgrinder tracks units removed from the battlefield by turn to resolve its kill comparisons and only awards the opponent-home bonus from round 2 onward. Reconnaissance Sweep scores distinct table quarters, enemy units destroyed, objectives and its end-battle objective threshold. End-battle VP follows the Event Companion FAQ and is outside the 15VP-per-round cap, but remains subject to the 45VP primary match cap. Secure Asset uses the shared 11e action eligibility and interruption flow; the action excludes the player's home objective and completes at end of turn only while the unit still controls its selected objective. Control resolves before scoring, and scoring checkpoints cannot be applied twice.

Fixed Engage evaluates whole units in distinct quarters, more than six inches from the centre, excluding Aircraft, Battle-shocked and off-field models. An attached unit counts as one unit. Fixed Assassination scores newly destroyed enemy Character **models** at either player's turn end, including the wounds-characteristic bonus; surviving bodyguards do not prevent that score. Destroyed Characters cannot currently be revived, so the persisted destroyed-model ledger is scoped to that restriction.

Primary and secondary totals each have their current 45VP match cap and normally have a 15VP-per-round cap; end-battle scoring is exempt from the per-round cap. Each fixed secondary card also has its 20VP cap. At the end of five complete rounds, apply declared Battle Ready bonuses and compare final VP. Wiping an army does not end the game early. Randomized first-player order is preserved when advancing turns and rounds and granting battle-round-start Captain CP.
