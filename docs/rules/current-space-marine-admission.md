# Current Space Marine Chapter admission

Implementation status: the current-reference roster gate, six supported Chapter-locked detachment restrictions, current Dark Angels conditional Chapter keywords and the bounded Black-winged Vigilance rule are implemented. Focused local checks are recorded; human playtest remains pending. These checks do not certify a complete legal army.

## Rule and source

The selected [Wahapedia 11th-edition Space Marines faction page](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/) states that when an Adeptus Astartes datasheet has a second Faction keyword, that keyword names its Chapter, and an army cannot include units from more than one Chapter. The page lists the relevant app profiles at v2.8.1 (October 2026). On 28 October 2026, the Chapter restrictions were rechecked for the six supported Chapter-locked detachments: Wrath of the Rock, Darkflight Pursuit and Inner Circle Task Force require Dark Angels; Fist of the God-Emperor and Vow-sworn Crusaders require Black Templars; and Spearpoint Task Force requires White Scars. Each permits its named Chapter and bars other Adeptus Astartes Chapter units. Medusa's Wrath and Blade of Ultramar are grouped under Iron Hands and Ultramarines, respectively, but their current detachment rules do not state this restriction, so grouping alone does not force a Chapter context. The same page supplies the Ravenwing and Deathwing conditional keyword grants. Darkflight Pursuit also gives RAVENWING FLY units Ignores Cover on ranged attacks and makes OUTRIDER SQUAD units BATTLELINE.

Datasheet keyword facts are checked against the verified local Wahapedia Data Export snapshot `20261021T130623Z`, exported 21 October 2026 at 16:06:23 GMT+3 and retrieved at 19:46:58 UTC. The `Datasheets_keywords.csv` source file has SHA-256 `bdb2431dd3a24d34ec91e1aa1e7ddae9da3565faa18a56abcd8ead5247b4e341`; the full file manifest is preserved in the local ignored snapshot. The runtime Chapter keywords are represented in [the current Marine reference profiles](../../scripts/edition_11/current_reference_marine_profiles.gd). Conditional DEATHWING/RAVENWING grants are sourced from the Space Marines faction page above; the locally recorded identities and export context remain in [the keyword-context record](../wahapedia-keyword-context.json) and [the Marines source slice](../wahapedia-marines-slice.md).

## Runtime boundary

`ArmyCapabilities.admit()` applies the gate to Space Marines profiles marked `current_reference`. Explicit Chapter faction keywords select that Chapter for the list. A second, different Chapter returns an admission error. Shared ADEPTUS ASTARTES profiles without a Chapter faction keyword remain usable alongside the selected Chapter. `DEATHWING` and `RAVENWING` are non-Faction keywords granted by Dark Angels rules; they do not identify a unit's Chapter on their own. Prepared-match creation resolves their contextual values for current-reference Marine profiles even when current unit abilities are disabled; explicit Chapter selections and detachment effects remain gated by that option. A source profile containing multiple represented Chapter faction keywords fails closed.

The Army Workshop's Add units tab derives a preview from the same current profiles. After the draft contains a Chapter-specific unit, it hides other Chapter-specific choices while keeping generic Adeptus Astartes units visible. A mixed draft shows a removal instruction and hides additional Chapter-specific choices. The Add action checks again before changing the draft; match admission remains authoritative. Non-Faction Ravenwing/Deathwing rule keywords do not set the preview's Chapter.

During prepared-match creation, `ChapterRules.required_chapter_for_detachment()` is the single map for the required Chapter and display name of each supported Chapter-locked detachment. `CombatDoctrines.chapter_restriction_error()` uses it to check selected context and explicit Chapter faction keywords in current profiles. The restrictions cover Dark Angels, Black Templars and White Scars detachments listed above. Shared Adeptus Astartes profiles without a Chapter remain eligible when used with their matching Chapter context.

With current unit abilities enabled, each player can select a Chapter context or infer it from named roster profiles and the selected detachment. A named profile that conflicts with the selected Chapter is rejected; each of the six Chapter-locked detachments implies its required Chapter when no Chapter is otherwise represented. A selected context applies to generic current-reference ADEPTUS ASTARTES profiles. At prepared-match setup, source-projected DEATHWING and RAVENWING keywords are removed from shared profile instances before conditional Chapter rules are applied, regardless of the current-unit-abilities option. In Dark Angels context, TERMINATOR and DREADNOUGHT profiles and the source-listed Bladeguard Ancient, Bladeguard Veteran, Sternguard Veteran, Vanguard Veteran with Jump Packs, Land Raider variants and Repulsor variants receive DEATHWING; Azrael and Belial retain the DEATHWING keyword on their Chapter datasheets. MOUNTED profiles and VEHICLE+FLY profiles receive RAVENWING. The in-match datasheet shows effective keywords and calls out conditional Dark Angels grants. These keywords do not establish a Chapter faction identity. Historical or unreconciled profiles receive no conditional grants. The resolved context is recorded in the match manifest for saves and LAN peers; saved army setup retains the selected Chapter values when a saved roster is present.

For roster copy limits, Darkflight Pursuit's BATTLELINE grant to Outrider Squad is applied during prepared-match admission and uses the same source-keyed grant as materialized unit keywords. The focused context test passed within `.tools/verification/20261028T075538220026Z/results.json`. Other Chapter-specific unit rules, attachment combinations, equipment options and army-construction rules are not validated by this gate. Necrons and legacy drafts are outside its scope. Broader Chapter restriction checks and human playtest remain pending. Network validation is deferred to final integration. General army legality remains uncertified.
