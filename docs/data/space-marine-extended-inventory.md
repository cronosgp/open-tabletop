## Current rules and legality boundary

The current [Wahapedia Space Marines 11e datasheet index](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/datasheets.html) includes the Legends and Forge World entries in the 11e faction context. The official [Warhammer 40,000 downloads page](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/downloads/warhammer-40000/) lists a Space Marines Faction Pack updated 14 October 2026, three Chapter Legends packs updated 30 September 2026, and a Munitorum Field Manual updated 14 October 2026. The 11e Space Marines Faction Pack includes separate Imperial Armour and Legends datasheet appendices. These current sources mean the entries must not be dismissed as obsolete solely because they are labelled Legends or Forge World; they still need their own sheet-by-sheet data, points, composition, faction access and game-mode checks. Event organisers may impose additional restrictions.
