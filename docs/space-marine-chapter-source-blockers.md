## Wolf Guard Headtakers — `000004131`

The selected Wahapedia composition requires 3–6 Hunting Wolves and 3–6 Wolf Guard Headtakers. Its cost table and local Data Export repeat both six-model prices without identifying the model mix. The official [Munitorum Field Manual](https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en/space-wolves) now labels all four mixes. This maps all four MFM cost rows: the two mixed rows match the selected Wahapedia minimum, while the Headtakers-only rows do not. Although Wahapedia allows 3–6 models of each role, MFM v1.0 supplies no explicit price row for other role-count combinations; runtime admits only the exact priced 3+3 and 6+6 mixes and leaves other mixes unpriced. Its six source-listed Leader endpoints are selectable for the admitted profiles. See the [Headtakers sheet audit](marine-wolf-guard-headtakers.md) and [machine-readable source/hash audit](marine-wolf-guard-headtakers-audit.json).

Update, 29 October 2026: the official [Munitorum Field Manual](https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en/space-wolves) labels the duplicated cost rows by model mix, resolving the cost mapping:

| MFM-listed composition | Models | 1st–2nd unit cost | 3rd+ unit cost | Matches Wahapedia composition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3 Wolf Guard Headtakers | 3 | 85 pts | 95 pts | No |
| 3 Wolf Guard Headtakers + 3 Hunting Wolves | 6 | 115 pts | 125 pts | Yes |
| 6 Wolf Guard Headtakers | 6 | 170 pts | 180 pts | No |
| 6 Wolf Guard Headtakers + 6 Hunting Wolves | 12 | 230 pts | 240 pts | Yes |

The Headtakers-only options conflict with Wahapedia's selected minimum composition of 3–6 models of each type. Only the exact mixed 3+3 and 6+6 configurations have MFM costs and fit both sources. The 3- and 6-Headtaker-only rows conflict with the Wahapedia minimum; other role-count combinations allowed by the broad 3–6 composition range have no explicit MFM cost row. Runtime admits the two priced compatible mixes and does not infer costs for the rest.

The structurally valid subject slice records both model profiles, four weapon profiles, role-specific composition, the shield replacement, all four abilities and all six Leader endpoint identities with source-file hashes. Local integration covers the two admitted sizes, role stats/weapons, each ability, independent post-split quarry marks, all six public Leader attachments and construction exposure of only the two priced compatible mixes and rejection of incompatible or unpriced mixes. Current page/export and MFM v1.0 checks are complete for this sheet; exact oval-base geometry, human playtesting and full roster legality remain open. See the [Headtakers sheet audit](marine-wolf-guard-headtakers.md).
