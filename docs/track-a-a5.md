## Closed for these lists

- **Command Re-roll (15.02)** — Advance, Charge, hit, wound, save, damage, hazard, attack-count and Desperate Escape. Checked matches enable every implemented window.
- **Fire Overwatch (15.08)** — end of opponent Movement; one visible enemy within 24 inches; snap shooting (unmodified 6s, not critical hits, no hit rerolls). The reacting unit cannot start an action until that Movement phase ends.
- **Heroic Intervention (15.11)** — end of opponent Charge. Leap to Defend (1 CP, only against units that declared a charge) or Into the Fray (+1 CP, charge roll capped at 6).
- **Counteroffensive (15.12)** — opponent Fight phase, 2 CP, just after an enemy unit resolves attacks; the chosen unit Fights First next.
- **Epic Challenge (15.03)** — Character unit selected to fight; that Character model's melee weapons gain Precision.
- Insane Bravery 15.04 / Rapid Ingress 15.07 — already implemented for these lists.
