# Land Raider — source-bounded local scope

This closes the shared-book 11th-edition local datasheet scope for [Land Raider](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/space-marines/Land-Raider), source book 000000139, datasheet 000000065. The live page and verified local Wahapedia Data Export snapshot were checked on 28 October 2026. The checked context is the common 40K datasheet and points view, with no Chapter selected; this scope does not certify Boarding Actions or other mode-specific lists.

## Implemented and checked

- The local profile records the one-model composition, 245-point first copy and 265-point second-and-later copies from the source's distinct cost sections. Roster admission and summaries apply the correct tier for each copy; the current local catalog caps this datasheet at three copies.
- The profile retains the source characteristics, keywords, default Godhammer Lascannons, Twin Heavy Bolter and Armoured Tracks. Optional Hunter-killer Missile, Multi-melta and Storm Bolter choices are reversible during Army Preparation, retain One Shot where sourced, and add no points.
- The shared transport rules allow up to 14 ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY spaces, count GRAVIS and TERMINATOR models twice, and reject JUMP PACK passengers. Assault Ramp permits the source-listed assault disembark after a normal move, with its tested 3-inch placement and charge eligibility.
- Damaged 6 and Deadly Demise D6 are recorded from the source. Power of the Machine Spirit can pause ranged attack resolution for one Hit reroll and one Wound reroll. The choice is exposed to the authorized player, resumes the pending attack, and remains once per attack activation across multiple targets.
- Profile, loadout, machine-spirit, transport, destruction, admission, saved-roster pricing and Army Builder regressions passed in the focused offline run recorded in the [machine-readable source and hash audit](marine-land-raider-audit.json).

## Limits

The source gives the vehicle's base as “Use model”; runtime uses a labelled 150 mm circular proxy, not exact model geometry. The three-copy catalog ceiling is a local construction rule, not the complete current-edition army limit. The source has no shared-book Leader or Support links for this datasheet. Chapter-book content, full army legality, network behavior, complete-match certification and human playtesting remain outside this scope; network validation is deferred to final integration.
