## Implemented and checked

- The local profile records the one-model composition, 245-point first copy and 265-point second-and-later copies from the source's distinct cost sections. Roster admission and summaries apply the correct tier for each copy; the current local catalog caps this datasheet at three copies.
- The profile retains the source characteristics, keywords, default Flamestorm Cannons, Twin Assault Cannon and Armoured Tracks. Optional Hunter-killer Missile, Multi-melta and Storm Bolter choices are reversible during Army Preparation, retain One Shot where sourced, and add no points.
- The shared transport rules allow up to 14 ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY spaces, count GRAVIS and TERMINATOR models twice, and reject JUMP PACK passengers. Damaged 6, Deadly Demise D6, and the sourced transport abilities remain attached to this profile.
- Wrath of the Machine Spirit adds Devastating Wounds to the Redeemer's ranged attacks against eligible targets within 12 inches. Focused attack-plan checks cover the in-range case and exclude MONSTER and VEHICLE targets.
- Profile, loadout, transport, destruction, admission, saved-roster pricing, Army Builder and Redeemer ability regressions passed in the focused offline run recorded in the [machine-readable source and hash audit](marine-land-raider-redeemer-audit.json).
