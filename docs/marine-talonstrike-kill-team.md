## Profile and equipment

The ten-model unit contains one Kill Team Sergeant, four Kill Team Heavy Intercessors and five Kill Team Intercessors, all with Jump Packs. The Sergeant and Intercessors use M12", T5, Sv3+, W2, Ld6+, OC1 and 32 mm bases. Heavy Intercessors use M10", T6, Sv3+, W3, Ld6+, OC1 and 40 mm bases. The unit costs 280 points for its first two copies and 300 points for its third copy, with a three-copy limit.

The Sergeant carries a Chainsword and Heavy Bolt Pistol by default. The Sergeant may replace its Chainsword with a Power Fist or Power Weapon, and its Heavy Bolt Pistol with a Hand Flamer or Plasma Pistol. One Intercessor per five models may replace its Heavy Bolt Pistol with a Plasma Pistol. Each Heavy Intercessor may replace its Assault Bolters with Plasma Exterminators. All choices are reversible through the public unit-preparation command path. Ranged and melee attacks retain their source profiles, including both Plasma Pistol and Plasma Exterminator modes.
